About me.

Hello! My name is Gerardo Murga.

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Economics and a Computer Science Minor.

My experiences have revolved around my life’s goal of building a great company in Latin America. As part of the journey, I’ve acquired strong finance and data science / ML engineering skills.

  • I currently work as an Econometrics & ML Research Assistant in Stanford’s Department of Economics, supporting the development of a statistical model.
  • 2025 Intern, Corporate & Investment Banking (Operations and M&A rotation) at BBVA Mexico. I generated annual savings of millions of pesos by automating the auditing of over 14,000 permits, identifying around 800 allocation errors.
  • I am a Rise fellow (Rhodes Trust and Schmidt Futures). We were named by Forbes among the world’s 100 most promising change-makers. Within Rise, I founded the Rise Latin American Union and collaborated with Latin American partners for strategic outreach in the region.
